Defend Earth against relentless waves of fire and ice asteroids by controlling two orbiting shields that constantly rotate around the planet. One shield represents the blazing Sun while the other embodies the frozen Moon, and success depends on matching every incoming asteroid with its opposite element. Block ice asteroids with the fire shield and fire asteroids with the ice shield while avoiding disastrous elemental collisions. As the action intensifies, asteroid speed and shield rotation increase, demanding sharper reflexes, precise timing, and quick decision-making. Chain perfect blocks to build score multipliers, collect powerful bonuses including extra shields, slow motion, rotation stabilizers, and instant score boosts, then unleash the Pulse ability to clear the screen with a powerful shockwave. Explore five handcrafted sectors featuring unique visual themes and escalating difficulty, monitor detailed statistics such as high score, survival time, longest combo, successful blocks, misses, collected power-ups, and Pulse usage, unlock achievements, and enjoy polished space visuals with intuitive controls designed for exciting skill-based gameplay
